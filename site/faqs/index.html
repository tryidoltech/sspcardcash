General

What services does SSP Card Cash provide?

SSP Card Cash provides credit card assistance in Lucknow, including bill payment assistance, card-to-card transfer guidance, credit card to bank transfer assistance, credit card to UPI assistance, cash assistance and credit limit guidance.

Is SSP Card Cash a bank or a lender?

Services

How does the credit card bill payment assistance process work?

You share your card type, bill amount and due date. We explain the applicable payment route, timeline and our charges. Once you agree, we guide you through the payment and you verify the update in your card app or statement.

How does credit card to bank transfer assistance work?

We understand your requirement and explain the available routes, including any issuer options, along with timelines and total costs. Funds can only go to a bank account in the cardholder's own name.

How does credit card to UPI assistance work?

We check whether your card can be used on UPI, such as RuPay credit cards on supported apps, and guide you through setup or the applicable process. We never ask for your UPI PIN or OTP.

Can SSP Card Cash increase my credit limit?

No. Only your card issuer can change your limit. We provide guidance on how limit reviews work and how to request one through official channels.

Do you guarantee approval, savings or a better CIBIL score?

No. We do not guarantee approvals, savings, credit score changes or any specific financial outcome. We explain options honestly so you can make an informed decision.

Charges, Documents & Safety

What documents or information may be required?

Usually your active credit card in your own name, a valid government photo ID and your registered mobile number. Some services may need a bank account in your own name. We tell you exactly what is needed for your case.

What are your charges?

Charges depend on the service, the amount and the route. We explain our service charge clearly before you proceed. Card issuer fees and interest are separate and set by your issuer.

Will SSP Card Cash ever ask for my OTP or PIN?

No. We will never ask for your card PIN, UPI PIN, OTP or CVV over a call or message. Please do not share these with anyone.

Can I get assistance for someone else's card?

No. We assist only the cardholder, for cards issued in their own name, after basic verification.

How is my personal information used?

Your details are used only to respond to your enquiry and provide the service you request. We do not sell your data. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
