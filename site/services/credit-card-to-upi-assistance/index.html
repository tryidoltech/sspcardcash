FAQs

Credit Card to UPI Assistance: frequently asked questions

Can I use my credit card on UPI?

RuPay credit cards can be linked with supported UPI apps. Other cards follow different rules. Share your card type and we will explain what is possible.

How do I link my RuPay credit card to UPI?

In most supported UPI apps you can add a RuPay credit card from the payment methods section and verify it with your card details and OTP. We can guide you through the screens if you get stuck, without ever asking for your OTP or PIN.

Are there charges for using a credit card on UPI?

Charges depend on the issuer, the merchant and the type of transaction. We explain what we know about your case and the charge for our assistance, if any, before you proceed.

Can I send money to a friend through credit card UPI?

Credit card UPI is mainly meant for merchant payments, and person-to-person transfers are generally not supported. Contact us and we will explain the options that apply to you.
