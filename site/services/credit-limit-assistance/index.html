What is credit limit assistance?

Credit limit assistance is a guidance service. It helps you understand how your credit limit is set, what usually influences limit reviews, and how to make a limit increase request through your card issuer's official channels.

Please note: credit limit decisions are made only by your card issuer. SSP Card Cash does not increase limits and does not influence approvals. What we offer is clarity, so you can make an informed request.

How SSP Card Cash helps

We explain the factors issuers commonly consider, such as repayment history, income documents and how much of your current limit you use. We show you where to find the limit increase option in your issuer's app or website and what information is usually requested.

We also help you plan your card usage so that you do not depend on a higher limit than you can comfortably repay.

FAQs

Credit Limit Assistance: frequently asked questions

Can SSP Card Cash increase my credit limit?

No. Only your card issuer can change your credit limit. We guide you on how limit reviews generally work and how to make the request through official channels.

What affects a credit limit review?

Issuers commonly look at repayment history, income, existing debt and how you use your current limit. The exact criteria are set by each issuer.

How do I request a higher limit?

Most issuers allow requests through their mobile app, net banking or customer care. Some offer pre-approved increases. We can show you where to look.

Is there a fee for this guidance?

Please contact us for details. We will tell you clearly if any charge applies before we begin.

Will a higher limit improve my credit score?

It may affect your utilisation ratio, but credit scores depend on many factors and we cannot promise any change. Timely repayment is the most important habit.
