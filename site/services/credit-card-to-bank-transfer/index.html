What is credit card to bank transfer assistance?

Credit card to bank transfer assistance is for cardholders who want to understand how the available limit on their credit card can be used to get funds into their own bank account. Common needs include urgent bills, medical expenses, fees or short-term business requirements.

Some issuers offer this directly through features like fund transfer or loan-on-card. Other routes may involve different charges. SSP Card Cash helps you understand what applies to your card and assists you through the process you choose.

How SSP Card Cash helps

We listen to your requirement, check the card type and the amount, and explain the available options in plain language. That includes the processing time, our service charge and any fees or interest your bank may apply.

If you decide to go ahead, we assist you through the steps and keep you updated until the process is complete.

General requirements

Important considerations

  • Your card issuer may treat certain transactions as cash advances, which can attract fees and interest from day one.

FAQs

Credit Card to Bank Transfer Assistance: frequently asked questions

Can I transfer money from my credit card to my bank account?

Many cardholders can, depending on the card issuer and the route used. Contact us and we will explain what may apply to your card, along with the costs involved.

Can the money go to someone else's account?

No. We assist only with transfers to a bank account held in the cardholder's own name.

What are the charges?

Charges depend on the amount, the card and the route. Our team tells you the full cost upfront, including our service charge, before you proceed.

Is it the same as a cash advance?

Not always, but some issuers may classify such transactions as cash advances. We recommend checking your cardholder agreement, and we will flag this where it is relevant.

What documents are needed?

Usually a valid photo ID and details of your bank account in your own name. We will tell you exactly what is needed for your case.
