Managing dues across two or more cards? SSP Card Cash helps you understand how a card-to-card transfer may work for you, what it costs and what to watch out for.

What is credit card to credit card transfer assistance?

Credit card to credit card transfer assistance helps cardholders who want to use the available limit on one card to manage the outstanding amount on another card. People usually look for this when one card's due date is close and another card has unused limit.

Every bank has its own rules on such transactions. Some issuers offer a formal balance transfer facility, while others may treat certain transactions differently. Our role is to explain the applicable route and help you move ahead only if it makes sense for you.

How SSP Card Cash helps

We start by understanding both cards, the amount involved and your timeline. We then explain the possible route, the expected processing time and the total cost, including our service charge and any fees your issuer may levy.

If the process suits you, we guide you through it step by step. If it does not, we will tell you that honestly.

Who can use this service?

  • Cardholders with more than one active credit card
  • Customers who want to understand balance transfer options from their issuer
  • Anyone who wants help comparing costs before moving dues between cards

General requirements

  • Both credit cards should be active and issued in your own name
  • Available limit on the card you plan to use
  • Outstanding amount and due date on the other card
  • A valid government photo ID, if required for verification

Important considerations

  • Moving dues between cards does not reduce the total amount you owe. Please borrow responsibly.
  • Your issuer may apply fees, interest or treat some transactions as cash advances. Check your card's terms.
  • Repeatedly rolling dues between cards can increase your overall cost. We will point this out where relevant.
  • We do not promise any specific financial outcome or impact on your credit score.

FAQs

Credit Card to Credit Card Transfer Assistance: frequently asked questions

What is credit card to credit card transfer?

It means using the available limit on one credit card to manage the outstanding amount on another. Some banks offer this as a formal balance transfer, and the rules vary from issuer to issuer.

Does this reduce my total debt?

No. It only shifts where the amount is owed. It can help with timing, but you still need to repay the full amount along with any applicable charges.

How much does it cost?

The cost depends on the cards involved, the amount and your issuer's terms. We explain our service charge and any likely issuer charges before you decide.

How long does the process take?

It depends on the issuer and the route used. We give you a realistic timeline after understanding your requirement.

Will this affect my CIBIL score?

Credit scores are calculated by credit bureaus based on many factors, including utilisation and repayment history. We cannot promise any change to your score. Paying on time is always the best habit.
