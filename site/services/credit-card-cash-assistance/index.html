What is credit card cash assistance?

Credit card cash assistance is for cardholders who want to access funds using their available credit limit. The most common direct option is an ATM cash advance, which usually carries a fee and interest from the day of withdrawal. Other routes may have different costs and conditions.

SSP Card Cash explains these routes in simple terms, compares the likely costs and assists you with the option you choose. We believe you should understand exactly what you are paying for.

How SSP Card Cash helps

We begin by understanding the amount you need, your card type and how soon you need the funds. We explain the available options, the timeline and the complete cost.

If you choose to proceed with our assistance, our team supports you through each step and confirms when the process is complete. You can also visit our office on Faizabad Road, near Indira Canal.

FAQs

Credit Card Cash Assistance: frequently asked questions

Can I get cash from my credit card?

Most credit cards allow an ATM cash advance up to a cash limit set by the issuer, usually with a fee and interest. Contact us to understand this and other options that may apply to your card.

What does it cost?

The cost depends on the route and the card. We clearly explain our service charge and the likely issuer charges before you decide.

How quickly can this be done?

Timelines vary with the route and the issuer. We give you a realistic estimate after understanding your requirement, and we never promise instant results.

Can someone else use my card on my behalf?

No. We assist only the cardholder, for their own card, after verification.
