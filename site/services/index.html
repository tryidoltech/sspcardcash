FAQs

Questions about our services

How does the credit card bill payment assistance process work?

You share your card type, bill amount and due date. We explain the applicable payment route, timeline and our charges. Once you agree, we guide you through the payment and you verify the update in your card app or statement.

How does credit card to bank transfer assistance work?

We understand your requirement and explain the available routes, including any issuer options, along with timelines and total costs. Funds can only go to a bank account in the cardholder's own name.

How does credit card to UPI assistance work?

We check whether your card can be used on UPI, such as RuPay credit cards on supported apps, and guide you through setup or the applicable process. We never ask for your UPI PIN or OTP.

Can SSP Card Cash increase my credit limit?

No. Only your card issuer can change your limit. We provide guidance on how limit reviews work and how to request one through official channels.

Do you guarantee approval, savings or a better CIBIL score?

No. We do not guarantee approvals, savings, credit score changes or any specific financial outcome. We explain options honestly so you can make an informed decision.
