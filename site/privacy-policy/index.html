Privacy Policy

How SSP Card Cash collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: 2026-11-20

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how SSP Card Cash ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects information when you visit our website, call us, message us on WhatsApp or visit our office in Lucknow. By using our website or services, you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to respond to your enquiry and provide the assistance you request. This may include:

  • Your name, mobile number and email address
  • The service you are interested in and your area or locality
  • Details you share in messages, calls or the enquiry form
  • Identity and verification details, only when a service requires them
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited, collected by our hosting provider or analytics tools if enabled

We do not ask for, and you should never share, your card PIN, UPI PIN, OTP or CVV.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and contact you about it
  • To explain and provide the service you request
  • To verify that the cardholder is the person requesting assistance
  • To maintain records required for our operations and applicable laws
  • To improve our website and customer service

WhatsApp and phone communication

When you contact us on WhatsApp or by phone, your messages are handled through those platforms and are also subject to their own privacy policies. Our enquiry form opens WhatsApp with your details pre-filled; nothing is sent until you press send.

Sharing of information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may share information only when required to deliver the service you have asked for, when required by law or a government authority, or to protect our legal rights.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss or misuse. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, or as required by applicable law.

Cookies

Our website uses minimal technology. If we use analytics or embedded services such as Google Maps, those services may set cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to legal requirements. Contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
