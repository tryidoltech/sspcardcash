About SSP Card Cash

A Lucknow-based credit card assistance service built on clear communication, honest guidance and convenient support.

Get AssistanceWhatsApp Us

Who we are

SSP Card Cash is a credit card assistance service based in Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh. Our office is at Shop No. 05, Ground Floor, Spring Greens, near Indira Canal on Faizabad Road, and we serve customers across the city by phone, WhatsApp and in person.

We started with a simple observation: many cardholders have questions about bill payments, transfers, UPI and limits, but find it hard to get a straight answer. We wanted to be the local team people could call for clear, honest guidance.

What we do

We help cardholders understand and complete everyday credit card tasks. Our services include credit card bill payment assistance, card-to-card transfer guidance, credit card to bank transfer assistance, credit card to UPI assistance, cash assistance and credit limit guidance.

Our customer-focused approach

We do not start by selling. We start by listening. Once we understand your requirement, we explain the options that may apply, the costs involved and any issuer rules you should know. If something is not right for you, we say so.

Serving all of Lucknow

Our customers come from every part of the city, from Gomti Nagar and Indira Nagar in the east to Alambagh and Rajajipuram in the west. Customers close to Faizabad Road, Chinhat and Kamta often walk in, while others prefer phone and WhatsApp.

What we will never do

  • Ask for your card PIN, UPI PIN, OTP or CVV
  • Promise guaranteed approvals, savings, cash or credit score improvement
  • Hide charges or add surprises after you agree
  • Assist with a card that is not in the customer's own name
