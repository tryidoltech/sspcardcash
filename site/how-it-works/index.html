From first message to completion

Step 1: Contact us

Reach out the way that suits you. Call +91 81003 58200, WhatsApp us on the same number, fill the enquiry form, or visit our office on Faizabad Road near Indira Canal.

Step 2: Share your requirement

Tell us what you need help with, your card type and your timeline. You do not need to share your card number, PIN, OTP or CVV at this stage, and you should never share PIN, OTP or CVV with anyone.

Step 3: Get assistance

Our team explains the applicable process, the expected timeline and the charges. If your issuer's rules or fees are relevant, we point them out. You can take your time and ask questions.

Step 4: Complete the process

If you decide to proceed, we follow the agreed process and keep you updated. Once complete, you can verify the status in your card app, statement or bank account.

What to keep ready

  • Your active credit card, issued in your own name
  • A valid government photo ID
  • Your registered mobile number
  • Bank account in your own name, where the service requires it
  • Your bill amount and due date, for bill-related services

Stay safe

  • Never share your card PIN, UPI PIN, OTP or CVV with anyone, including us.
  • Always confirm charges before you proceed.
  • Use credit responsibly and plan your repayments.

FAQs

Questions about the process

How can I request assistance?

Call us, send a WhatsApp message, or fill the enquiry form on our Contact page with your name, mobile number, the service you need and your area. Our team will get back to you.

What documents or information may be required?

Usually your active credit card in your own name, a valid government photo ID and your registered mobile number. Some services may need a bank account in your own name. We tell you exactly what is needed for your case.

What are your charges?

Charges depend on the service, the amount and the route. We explain our service charge clearly before you proceed. Card issuer fees and interest are separate and set by your issuer.

Will SSP Card Cash ever ask for my OTP or PIN?

No. We will never ask for your card PIN, UPI PIN, OTP or CVV over a call or message. Please do not share these with anyone.
